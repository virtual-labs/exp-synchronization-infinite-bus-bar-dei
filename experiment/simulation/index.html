Governing Principles

The angular speed ($\omega$) of a DC Shunt Motor is controlled by adjusting the external resistance in the armature circuit. The relationship is defined by:

$$\omega = \frac{V_t - I_a(R_{a_{int}} + R_{a_{ext}})}{K \phi}$$
  • As **$R_{a_{ext}}$** increases, the voltage drop across the armature circuit increases.
  • Since $V_t$ is constant, the **Back EMF ($E_b$)** must decrease.
  • Consequently, the **Speed** decreases linearly with the increase in resistance for a fixed load.
